Client Challenge

GENIE MD, a digital healthcare company, needed a secure and scalable telehealth platform to manage virtual consultations, chronic care, and remote patient monitoring. Their primary concerns were HIPAA compliance, integrating biometric devices, and ensuring uninterrupted care delivery across different geographies.

RPDC’s Solution

RPDC developed a comprehensive telehealth ecosystem that included separate portals for patients and care providers. The platform supports real-time video consultations, RPM (Remote Patient Monitoring), and RTM (Remote Therapeutic Monitoring), integrated with a wide array of medical wearables such as Fitbit, Apple Watch, and AliveCor.

We ensured seamless EHR/EMR integration and implemented secure payment gateways, keeping compliance and user experience at the forefront.

Features Delivered

  • Real-time HD video consultations
  • Biometric and remote monitoring tools (RPM, RTM)
  • Chronic Care Management workflows
  • Stripe and Nexio for payments
  • HIPAA-compliant cloud infrastructure

Business Impact

  • Expanded virtual care delivery at scale
  • Reduced appointment no-shows by enabling remote access
  • Improved chronic care adherence through continuous monitoring

Client Challenge

A healthcare compliance firm needed a platform to manage and monitor the credentials of medical staff across multiple hospitals and clinics. Their existing manual system was time-consuming and risked non-compliance with audits, leading to possible legal and reputational risks for client hospitals.

RPDC’s Solution

RPDC designed and developed GoPerla 2.0, a robust cloud-based compliance management platform. It automates credential tracking, sends timely expiration alerts, and allows for secure document uploads—all through a user-friendly interface.

Using modern frameworks like Next.js, NestJS, and GraphQL, we ensured performance, scalability, and security. The platform also integrates with AWS Cognito for user authentication and AWS services for storage and analytics.

Key Features

  • Automated tracking of certifications and license expirations
  • Timely renewal notifications and escalation alerts
  • Secure upload and access of credential documents
  • Real-time dashboards to monitor compliance status
  • Role-based access and AWS-integrated authentication

Business Impact

  • Achieved 100% audit readiness across client hospitals
  • Reduced admin workload by 70%
  • Improved visibility into staff compliance
  • Helped hospital networks stay aligned with regulatory standards
